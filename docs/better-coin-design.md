# Coin System Philosophy & Design Rules

## Purpose of Coins

Coins are not the primary source of complexity.

Scams are.

Coins exist to provide:

- Readable build pieces
- Synergy opportunities
- Draft decisions
- Pouch progression
- Black Market decisions

The player should be able to understand a coin immediately.

The player should not need to read tooltips repeatedly.

---

# Core Philosophy

## Coin Identity Must Be Obvious

A player should recognize a coin in less than a second.

Good:

- Bent Coin
- Hollow Coin
- Vanishing Coin
- Blank Coin
- Marked Coin
- Lucky Coin

Bad:

- Bent Counterfeit Lucky Coin
- Gold Heads Hunter Coin
- Hollow Smuggler Coin

The game should avoid stacking multiple permanent identities onto a single coin. Forgery Tricks may schedule temporary copied Outcomes or bounded activations during resolution, and Smuggling Tricks may create temporary physical contraband copies, but the coin's readable owned archetype stays singular.

---

## One Coin = One Archetype

Every coin should represent one clear idea.

Examples:

### Bent Coin

Prestige instability archetype.

---

### Flywheel Coin

Momentum archetype.

---

### Blank Coin

Forgery archetype.

---

### Vanishing Coin

Sleight of Hand archetype.

---

### Hollow Coin

Smuggling archetype.

---

### Marked Coin

Foretold/readable result archetype.

---

### Lucky Coin

Luck Meter / Fated Flip archetype.

---

The player should learn the archetype once and understand it forever.

---

## Coin Complexity Is Horizontal

New coins should introduce:

- Different interactions
- Different opportunities
- Different synergies

Not:

- Bigger numbers

---

Good:

Bent Coin supports encores and Prestige Outcome replays.

---

Good:

Flywheel Coin supports Momentum links.

---

Bad:

Bent Coin +5 Score.

---

Good:

Blank Coin benefits from Forgery Tricks.

---

Bad:

Blank Coin but stronger.

---

# Coin Hierarchy

Coins are divided into:

## Archetype

Defines what the coin does.

Examples:

- Bent
- Hollow
- Blank
- Marked
- Lucky
- Weighted

---

## Quality

Defines how strongly the archetype is expressed.

Examples:

- Copper
- Silver
- Gold

Quality should increase identity rather than replace it.

---

# Quality Rules

## Quality Means "More Of"

Quality should amplify the coin's fantasy.

Not its raw score.

---

Example:

### Bent Coin

Fantasy:

This coin does not stay resolved: it supports discounted Prestige Outcome replays.

---

Copper Bent

Baseline eligibility for Outcome replay effects.

---

Silver Bent

Stronger replay priority or premium Outcome-replay eligibility.

---

Gold Bent

Strongest finale priority or premium Outcome-replay eligibility.

---

Identity remains:

Bent.

---

Quality simply means:

More Bent.

---

# What Quality Should NOT Do

Bad:

Copper Bent = 2 Score

Silver Bent = 4 Score

Gold Bent = 8 Score

---

Result:

Player learns Gold.

Player ignores Bent.

---

The archetype becomes meaningless.

---

# Coin Categories

## Bent Coin

Theme:

Unstable.

Never truly stays resolved.

Good at supporting Prestige Outcome replays.

Preferred Builds:

- Prestige
- Encore

Good Everywhere:

Yes.

Exceptional In:

Prestige builds that care about completed Outcomes.

---

## Flywheel Coin

Theme:

Motion.

Keeps force moving through the flip.

Good at supporting Momentum links and In Motion payoffs.

Preferred Builds:

- Momentum
- Keep It Rolling
- Follow Through
- Ripple

Good Everywhere:

Medium.

Exceptional In:

Momentum builds.

---

## Blank Coin

Theme:

Forgery medium: an unstamped coin waiting for stolen credentials.

Mechanical Identity:

- activates Forgery Tricks through its real family;
- reads the genuine non-Forgery coin immediately to its left and locks that coin's family as its acting family before Flip;
- can copy part of that coin's completed Outcome through Fake Credentials;
- can imitate bounded Tricks from that coin's family through Borrowed Name;
- can repeat one valuable eligible Trick through Forged Signature;
- never permanently changes owned identity or real activation family;
- runs its bounded copied Trick package through pre-roll and post-result phases alike.

Preferred Builds:

- hybrid engines with an established primary family;
- deliberate left-to-right coin positioning;
- upgraded Borrowed Name and Forged Signature lines;
- compact pouches that can reliably draw a genuine source beside a Blank.

Risks:

A Blank without a genuine left source or compatible family Tricks provides no Forgery payoff. This is intentional opportunity cost, not a fallback that should silently choose another source.

## Hollow Coin

Theme:

Hand overflow.

Contraband capacity.

Good at getting extra real coins onto the board.

Easier to put onto the board when resolving the flip, easier to smuggle in.

Preferred Builds:

- Smuggling
- Hidden Pocket
- Hidden in Plain Sight
- Off the Books
- Planted Double
- Embarrassment of Riches

Good Everywhere:

Sometimes.

Exceptional In:

Smuggling builds that overload the board with extra hand coins or temporary contraband copies.

---

## Vanishing Coin

Theme:

Magician's coin.

Half-seen body.

Palms, swaps and impossible rearrangements.

Good at moving through already-resolved result slots without changing those results.

Preferred Builds:

- Sleight of Hand
- Switcheroo
- Vanishing Act
- Three-Card Monte

Good Everywhere:

Sometimes.

Exceptional In:

Sleight builds that need a coin body that is easy to palm, swap or rearrange.

---

## Marked Coin

Theme:

Information.

Foretold results.

Ancient Patterns.

Preferred Builds:

- Card Shark
- Prediction
- Foretold coins
- Ancient Patterns

Good Everywhere:

Sometimes.

Exceptional In:

Prediction builds that reveal and fulfill Foretold coins.

---

## Lucky Coin

Theme:

Luck Meter fuel.

Fated Flip payoff.

Destiny engine.

Preferred Builds:

- Fated Flip
- Luck Meter
- Fountain Favor
- Omen Engine
- Twist of Fate

Good Everywhere:

Usually.

Exceptional In:

Fate builds that fill the Luck Meter quickly, boost Fountain Favor or cash out Fated Flip payoffs.

---

## Weighted Coin

Theme:

Weight.

Commitment.

Reliability.

Preferred Builds:

- Weighted
- Weight
- Heads/Tails Specialists

Good Everywhere:

Yes.

Exceptional In:

Probability builds.

---

# Build Philosophy

## Coins Are Enablers

Coins should create opportunities.

Scams should exploit them.

---

Example:

Bent Coin

Provides unstable Outcome replay support.

---

Flywheel Coin

Provides live Momentum-link support.

---

Prestige

Exploits completed Outcomes.

---

The coin alone should not be the build.

---

The scam alone should not be the build.

---

The interaction should be the build.

---

# Build Alignment

Every coin should answer:

> "Which build wants me most?"

Example:

Bent -> Prestige / Outcome replay

Flywheel -> Momentum / In Motion links

Blank -> Forgery / copied Outcome and activation credentials

Hollow -> Smuggling

Marked -> Prediction / Foretold coins

Lucky -> Fate / Luck Meter / Fated Flip

Weighted -> Weighted / Weight

---

If every build wants the coin:

It is too generic.

---

If only one build can use the coin:

It is too narrow.

---

Goal:

Useful Everywhere.

Exceptional Somewhere.

---

# Heads/Tails Philosophy

Heads and Tails are not coin archetypes.

Heads and Tails are build directions.

---

Avoid:

Heads Coin

Tails Coin

Heads Hunter Coin

Tails Hunter Coin

as primary identity.

---

Instead:

Bent Coin can support a Heads build.

Blank Coin can support a Heads build.

Lucky Coin can support a Heads build.

---

Heads/Tails should exist primarily through:

- Scams
- Calls
- Weighted effects
- Probability manipulation

Not through coin identity.

---

# Coin Acquisition

## Black Market

Coins are primarily acquired through the Black Market.

The Black Market exists to:

- Improve the pouch
- Introduce new archetypes
- Upgrade coin quality
- Support evolving builds

---

The Black Market should present decisions.

Not obvious upgrades.

---

Good:

Copper Bent

Silver Hollow

Gold Blank

---

Interesting because the player evaluates synergy.

---

Bad:

Copper Coin

Silver Coin

Gold Coin

---

Player always buys the biggest number.

---

# Pouch Philosophy

The pouch is the player's coin collection for the current run.

The pouch should evolve throughout the run.

Players should:

- Add coins
- Remove coins
- Upgrade coins
- Refine coin composition

---

The pouch should eventually become a reflection of the player's chosen scams.

---

# Readability Rule

The player should be able to answer:

"What does this coin do?"

without opening a tooltip.

Examples:

Bent Coin -> encores and Outcome replays

Flywheel Coin -> Momentum links

Blank Coin -> forgery medium

Hollow Coin -> Smuggling

Vanishing Coin -> Sleight of Hand


Marked Coin -> foretold/readable results

Lucky Coin -> Luck Meter fuel

---

If a coin requires reading a paragraph to understand its identity:

The design has failed.

---

# Ultimate Coin Design Goal

The player should eventually think:

"I need a Bent Coin for this Prestige scam."

not

"I need the coin with +8 score."

The coin's value should come from how it interacts with the player's scam engine rather than from raw numerical power.
